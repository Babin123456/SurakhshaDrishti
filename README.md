# 🚨 Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 - Problem Statement

## 📋 Problem Details

- 🆔 **Problem Statement ID:** 26191
- 🏷️ **Problem Statement Title:** Intelligent Identification of Hazard-Based Red Zones, Carrying Capacity Assessment, and Immediate Relocation Needs for Vulnerable Habitations
- 🏛️ **Organization:** Ministry of Home Affairs
- 🛡️ **Department:** National Disaster Response Force (NDRF), DM Division
- 💻 **Category:** Software
- 🌐 **Theme:** Disaster Management

---

## 🌍 Background

India's disaster-prone regions face recurring hazards such as landslides 🏔️, floods 🌊, coastal erosion 🏖️, and cloudbursts ⛈️. Vulnerable habitations often remain in unsafe zones, leading to repeated loss of lives and property. Current relocation efforts are largely reactive, initiated after disasters strike, rather than proactively planned.

---

## 🎯 Problem Description

The initiative seeks to develop an intelligent, GIS-enabled decision support platform 🛰️. This platform will dynamically identify and update multi-hazard **Red Zones** (areas unsuitable for permanent habitation), assess the carrying capacity of safer alternative sites, and prioritize vulnerable habitations for relocation. The system will integrate hazard intensity, population vulnerability, and disaster history to guide evidence-based decisions.

---

## 💡 Expected Solution

A robust, AI-driven GIS platform that:
1. 🗺️ **Maps and Updates Red Zones:** Dynamically tracks multi-hazard Red Zones in real time.
2. ⚖️ **Carrying Capacity Assessment:** Assesses suitability and carrying capacity of safer relocation sites.
3. 🚨 **Relocation Prioritization:** Prioritizes vulnerable habitations for immediate, short-term, and medium-term relocation.
4. 📊 **Actionable Decision Support:** Provides actionable insights to State Disaster Management Authorities (SDMAs) for proactive disaster planning and mitigation.

---

## ⚡ Features (SIH26191)

> 💬 *"It's funny how we operate in a manner where we try to save someone by risking the lives of two, hoping all three could come back in one piece."*

1. 📡 **GSM 3.4 Integration for Telecommunication:** Resilient fallback telecommunication channels for low-connectivity disaster zones.
2. 🔐 **End-to-End Encrypted (E2EE) Communication:** Secure, authenticated channels for interdepartmental coordination.
3. 📍 **GeoHashed Locatives for Accuracy:** Precision geohashing algorithms for spatial indexing and rapid hazard mapping.
4. 👥 **Dual Role Integrations (User & Administrator):** Tailored operational views and workflows for ground users/citizens and disaster response administrators.
5. 🗺️ **Dynamic Heatmap Generation (Red, Yellow, & Green Zones):** Real-time multi-hazard risk categorization and predictive zoning.
6. 🛣️ **Custom Wayroutes and Communicatives for Reallocation of Persons:** Intelligent evacuation corridors, safe-zone routing, and logistical directives for habitations.
